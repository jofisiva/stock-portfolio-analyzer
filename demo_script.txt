DEMO SCRIPT - STOCK PORTFOLIO ANALYZER (target: 4.5 minutes, maximum 5)
=======================================================================

Setup before recording:
  - Run "streamlit run app.py".
  - Clear all filters.
  - Have two files ready:
      - an invalid CSV, for example a copy of the sample with "abc" as one
        quantity
      - a small valid CSV
  - Use only fictional data on screen.

0:00-0:30  THE PROBLEM
  Say: "Spreadsheets of stock holdings tell you what you own, but not how you
  are doing overall or where your money is concentrated. This app takes a CSV
  snapshot of Indian or US stock holdings, checks it, and answers: what have I
  invested, what is it worth now, which holdings and sectors dominate, and
  which are up or down?"
  Point to the info banner. Say: "Prices come from the CSV, not live data.
  Dividends, fees and taxes are not included."

0:30-1:15  THE AI-ASSISTED WORKFLOW
  Say: "I built this in five planned prompts with an AI coding assistant:
    1. load and validate the data
    2. add the metric cards
    3. add the charts
    4. add filters and export
    5. add the health view, review everything, and write the docs."
  Say: "Each prompt included acceptance checks. For example, an AAA/BBB example
  calculated by hand must give a 5% portfolio return. The assistant ran
  automated checks after every step. The final review found real bugs, such as
  a date like 2024-1-5 being accepted, and they were fixed."
  (Optional: show test_app.py running "all checks passed".)

1:15-1:45  SAMPLE DATA AND METRIC CARDS
  Point to the label "Sample data: all holdings and prices are fictional".
  Walk across the cards: total invested, current value, unrealized P&L with a
  sign and its portfolio return %, then the best and worst performer.
  Say: "Portfolio return is total P&L divided by total invested, not an average
  of individual returns."
  Switch Market to "USA ($ USD)". Say: "The same analysis works for US holdings,
  shown in dollars. Nothing is converted between currencies." Switch back.

1:45-2:30  CHARTS
  Hover over a donut slice to show the ticker, current value and weight.
  Hover over a sector bar.
  Point to the P&L chart. Say: "It is sorted from highest to lowest, green for
  gains and red for losses, with signed money labels."

2:30-3:15  FILTERS
  - Set Status to "Loss". Point out "4 of 10" and "Filters active". Say: "The
    cards, charts and table all update together."
  - Narrow the Buy date range. Say: "This selects holdings by purchase date. It
    does not show historical performance."
  - Type "zzz" in the search box to show the empty-result message, then click
    Reset filters.
  - Click "Download filtered analysis CSV" (optional).

3:15-4:00  UPLOAD AND VALIDATION
  - Upload the invalid CSV. Show the row-numbered errors. Say: "The app rejects
    the whole file and does not quietly fall back to the sample data."
  - Upload the valid CSV. Point out the label "Uploaded data: <file>" and that
    the filters reset.
  - Remove the uploaded file to return to the sample data.

4:00-4:40  PORTFOLIO HEALTH
  Scroll to "Portfolio Health: concentration analysis".
  Point out the largest holding and its weight, the top three's combined
  weight, the largest sector, and the gain/loss/unchanged counts.
  Read the summary aloud. Say: "This text is written by fixed rules from the
  calculated figures. It is a concentration view, not a risk score, and it
  gives no buy or sell advice."

4:40-5:00  WRAP-UP
  Say: "Next steps would need more data. Historical prices would allow
  performance and volatility, and transaction history would allow multiple buy
  lots and realized P&L. Thanks for watching."
